Sources & coverage

Openings come directly from the employer job boards listed below. This is an expanding set of employer feeds, not every U.S. vacancy. Positions must explicitly concern mechanical engineering, mechanisms, mechatronics, or electromechanical engineering and publish a base-salary minimum of at least $100,000 USD per year. Internships and hourly rates are excluded.

Employer feeds are scheduled to refresh every 30 minutes; GitHub scheduling can be delayed. This page checks the latest published feed every 30 minutes and when you return to the tab. Greenhouse “updated” dates are not treated as posting dates. The default sort favors energy roles within each age band; choose Newest listed for strict date order. Undated listings sort last. Energy labels reflect a verified employer sector or explicit job-title wording, not an assessment of your qualifications. Location pins are city approximations. U.S.-remote jobs without a specific location remain in the list.
