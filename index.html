Sources & coverage

Openings come directly from the employer job boards listed below. This is an expanding set of employer feeds, not every U.S. vacancy. Positions must explicitly concern mechanical engineering, mechanisms, mechatronics, or electromechanical engineering and publish a base-salary minimum of at least $100,000 USD per year. Internships and hourly rates are excluded.

Employer feeds are scheduled to refresh every 30 minutes; GitHub scheduling can be delayed. This page checks the latest published feed every 30 minutes and when you return to the tab. Greenhouse “updated” dates are not treated as posting dates. Newest listed is the default, with the most recent posting dates first. Undated listings sort last. The optional energy filter uses verified employer sectors or explicit job-title wording. Location pins are city approximations. U.S.-remote jobs without a specific location remain in the list.
